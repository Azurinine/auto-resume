Microsoft — Software Engineering Intern, Full-Stack Product (Summer)

Overview
As a Software Engineering Intern – Full-Stack Product, you will build end-to-end product experiences across user interfaces, APIs, and backend services. Collaborate with engineers, designers, and product managers to create intuitive, accessible, high-quality experiences. Contribute across the full development lifecycle: designing responsive user interfaces, building scalable services, testing, debugging, and iterating on features based on customer feedback. Strengthen your full-stack engineering skills while delivering experiences that impact millions of users.

Responsibilities
- Apply engineering principles to solve complex problems through sound and creative engineering.
- Work with stakeholders to determine user requirements for a feature.
- Quickly learn new engineering methods and incorporate them into work processes.
- Seek feedback and apply internal or industry best practices to improve technical solutions.
- Demonstrate time management and complete software projects in a cooperative team environment.
- Improve availability, reliability, efficiency, observability, and performance of products; drive consistency in monitoring and operations at scale.

Required Qualifications
- Currently pursuing a Bachelor's Degree in Computer Science or related technical field with at least one semester/term remaining following the completion of the internship,
- OR pursuing a Master's Degree in CS or related field with at least one semester/term remaining after the internship.

Preferred Qualifications
- 1+ year(s) of programming experience.
- 6+ months of experience delivering projects in teams.
- 1+ year(s) of experience developing and applying data structures and algorithms.
- Coursework, projects, or prior experience building end-to-end applications.
- Experience with TypeScript or JavaScript and modern web frameworks such as React or Angular.
- Experience building backend services using C#, Java, Node.js, Python, or similar technologies.
- Familiarity with REST APIs, testing frameworks, accessibility principles, and modern software engineering practices.
